Shin Translator - Credits & third-party attributions
====================================================

Shin Translator is an all-in-one visual novel translator. It is a modified
version (fork) built on the following open-source projects. Full credit goes to
their authors. Shin Translator adds/modifies the user interface, a screen OCR
mode, Japanese-learning helpers and several quality-of-life improvements.
